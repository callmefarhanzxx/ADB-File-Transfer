# 📱 ADB File Transfer 🚀
*Fast. Wireless. Bulletproof.*

**ADB File Transfer** is a lightweight, ultra-reliable desktop tool designed to seamlessly back up massive folders from your Android phone to your PC, or transfer folders from your PC to your phone. 

[![Platform](https://img.shields.io/badge/Platform-Windows%20%7C%20Android-0078D6?logo=windows&logoColor=white)](https://github.com/callmefarhanzxx/ADB-File-Transfer)
[![Releases](https://img.shields.io/badge/Releases-GitHub-blue?logo=github)](https://github.com/callmefarhanzxx/ADB-File-Transfer/releases)

---

Powered by a custom **Native ADB Engine**, it bypasses the slow speeds, 4GB limits, and frequent crashes of traditional cable transfers. Just connect via USB or Wi-Fi, and transfer 100GB+ of data without a single freeze or corrupted file.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

- 🚀 **High-Speed Transfer:** Runs at maximum speeds (50MB/s - 100MB/s+) to handle tens of thousands of files instantly without freezing.
- 🔄 **Smart Auto-Resume:** Cable disconnected mid-transfer? The engine automatically resumes multi-GB files exactly where they left off. No need to start from 0%.
- 🛡️ **Zero Data Corruption:** Every file undergoes strict **Byte-by-Byte Verification** to ensure 100% safe backup. 
- 📁 **Auto Filename Sanitizer:** Automatically fixes illegal Windows characters (`: * ? " < > |`) on the fly so transfers never crash. 
- 🛜 **Wireless Freedom:** Say goodbye to cables! Auto-fetch your IP and perform your entire backup over your local Wi-Fi network.
- 💻 **100% Portable (No Install):** Ready-to-use standalone `.exe`. No installations required. Works smoothly on Windows 8, 10, and 11.

## 🚀 How to Use (Step-by-Step)

### Step 1: Phone Setup (One-time)
1. Go to **Settings > About Phone** on your Android device.
2. Tap on **Build Number** 7 times consecutively until you see "You are now a developer!".
3. Go back to Settings, open **Developer Options**, and enable **USB Debugging**.

### Step 2: Connecting to PC
- **Cable Mode (Wired):** Connect your phone to your PC using a data cable. If a prompt appears on your phone screen, check *"Always allow from this computer"* and tap **Allow/OK**. The app will display a green 🟢 "Connected" status.
- **Wireless Mode (Wi-Fi):** 
  1. *Important:* Both devices must be on the **exact same Wi-Fi network**.
  2. First, connect your phone via USB cable to activate the wireless ADB port.
  3. The app will automatically fetch your phone's IP address.
  4. Click **Connect**. Once you see the "Connection Successful" pop-up, you can safely unplug the USB cable and enjoy fully wireless transfers!

## 🛠️ System Requirements
- A Windows PC (Windows 8, 10, or 11).
- An Android Smartphone.
- A stable Wi-Fi network (only required for Wireless Mode).

---

## ⚖️ Privacy & Security
This software is 100% safe, offline, and respects your data privacy. It acts simply as a secure, direct bridge between your own PC and your Android device. **No data is collected, monitored, or sent to any external servers.**
